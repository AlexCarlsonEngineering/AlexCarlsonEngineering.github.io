Spinner battlebot

ME 366J Senior Design I · team of six

Our first combat robot. We worked through customer interviews, a House of Quality, a morph matrix and a Pugh chart before landing on a spinning weapon driven by pulleys instead of gears, since a belt can slip and absorb a hit where gear teeth would break. The side plates were milled from 6061 and pocketed down to 2.7 oz each.

Final presentation (PDF)

Basketball shooter

Robotic Mechanisms Design · Spring 2024 · team of four

An automated version of a desktop basketball toy. A four-bar linkage throws the ball, and a button changes the motor speed so it can shoot to different spots on a 2 ft court. We worked backwards from target trajectories to get the link lengths and input speeds.
