04 / About

Hi, I'm Alex.

I'm a mechanical engineer who likes starting from first principles, getting my hands on the hardware, and designing for the person who has to use it.

It started on my high school FIRST Robotics team, where I was a mechanical design lead and ran scouting and strategy. That led to Mechanical Engineering at UT Austin (Engineering Honors, CS minor), then Texas Combat Robotics, and now Shield AI, where my designs go out into the field.
